01 ABOUT THE ANALYZER

Football is more than goals and assists.

The way you move, receive the ball, pass, make decisions and create space all contribute to the way you play.

FPA turns those characteristics into a structured football profile that helps you understand your strengths, playing tendencies and areas for development.
